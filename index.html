01 / Our point of view

Good tiling is felt in every detail.

A well-set tile floor brings calm to a room. From surface preparation and careful layout to clean cuts, grout and finishing, we deliver precise residential and commercial tilework.

Plan a tiling project ↗

We review the space, measure carefully, discuss tile choices and agree on a clear scope and schedule.

We check levels and substrate condition, then prepare a clean, stable surface ready for tile installation.

We set tiles to the agreed layout, checking alignment, spacing, cuts and edges as work progresses.

We grout and clean the tiled surfaces, then walk through the finished work and note any final details.
